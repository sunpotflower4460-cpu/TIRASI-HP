TIRASI Live Flyer Maker
Privacy Policy

This app does not collect personal information. The developer runs no server that receives your data.

Information kept on your device

The flyers you make, the images you import and the app settings are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see them.

Deleting the app also deletes this data. If you back up your device (for example with iCloud), the data may be included in that backup.

Photos and camera

When you use Save to Photos, the app asks for permission to add to your photo library. It does not list or read the photos already there.

When importing an image, only the picture you choose or take is used. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo.

Network use and third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. No information is provided to third parties.

Apple (the App Store) handles purchases and restoring purchases. Your payment details are never given to the developer.

If you open an external link or specify an external image, your device connects to that site, and its own policy applies.

Sharing and exporting

Sharing, printing and saving to files happen only when you ask, and only to the destination you choose.

A backup with images contains the original images you imported. If an image carries information such as where it was taken, that is included too. Take care where you share it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page and the text shown in the app are updated.
