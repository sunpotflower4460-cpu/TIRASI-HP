Frequently asked questions

I bought a plan but designs are still locked
Tap the plan button at the top right, then "Restore purchases". You need to be signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

I cannot save to Photos
In the iPhone Settings app, open "Live Flyer" and allow adding to Photos. You can also save with "Save to Files / Share".

I want to move my work to a new phone
Under "Save & backup" at the bottom of the screen, export a full backup and restore it on the new phone.

Refunds
Apple handles purchases. Request a refund by following Apple's instructions.
